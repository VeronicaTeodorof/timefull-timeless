Enel Dumitrascu

Sculptor Enel Dumitrascu was born in Braila, Romania, in 1975. He is a member of the Romanian Fine Artists' Union. He began his art studies at "H.H. Darclee" Art High School in Brăila; he then went to Bucharest to study metal sculpture at the National University of Arts in Prof. Bogdan Hojbotă's class. In 2004 he moved to Italy, where he completed a one year master's degree in sculpture at the Rome University of Fine Arts (R.U.F.A.) under Prof. Alfio Mongelli.

His works use a range of materials and techniques, including stone, Carrara marble, wood, plaster casting, and hammered and joined metal. He has also experimented with engraving techniques such as etching (aquaforte), aquatint, lithography, watercolor, pen-and-ink, and drypoint (point-sèche), while in painting he works in both acrylic and oil. His work explores several recurring themes over time: flight, knots, seed, angels - rendered both in drawing and in three-dimensional form.
