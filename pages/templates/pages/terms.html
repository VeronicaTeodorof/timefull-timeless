Terms of Purchase

These terms apply to purchases delivered within the United Kingdom or Romania, our two established delivery destinations. If you would like your sculpture delivered elsewhere, please contact us - we are happy to consider this on a case-by-case basis, but cannot guarantee insurance terms, or customs treatment described below for destinations outside the UK and Romania.

Insurance

All sculptures are insured for the full value of your purchase while in transit, at a flat rate of 1.5% included in your purchase price. There is no separate insurance charge and no opt-out, so every order is fully covered against loss or damage from the moment it leaves the studio until it reaches you.

Delivery

Orders are shipped within one business day of purchase.

You may choose between:

  • Studio pickup - no additional cost
  • Delivery - a flat shipping fee: £40 (UK), £15 (Romania)

Each sculpture is protected in a custom-built wooden crate during transit.

UK Customs & Import Charges

UK orders may be subject to import VAT and courier handling fees. These charges are not included in your purchase price and are the buyer's responsibility. Depending on the courier's own policy, this may be collected before delivery or invoiced afterward.

As an original artwork, each sculpture may qualify for reduced import VAT treatment - works of this kind are typically eligible for the UK's reduced 5% rate rather than the standard 20%, and may be exempt from customs duty. This is determined by HM Revenue & Customs at the time of import and is not guaranteed by us.
